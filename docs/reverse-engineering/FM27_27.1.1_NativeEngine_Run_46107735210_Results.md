The native scanner found 86 direct calls/jumps to the selected PI/role decision routines. In particular:

- The Half Back / dropping-DM gate is called from both \`0x35430\` and \`0x35CD0\`, as well as from the PI aggregator.

### Tracking Centre Forward is generated as a spatial candidate

\`0xA7280\` is called directly from \`0x4862CB0\`. It writes candidate coordinates, a distance/threshold value, candidate flag \`0x20000000\`, and OOP compact role ID \`0x58\` (Tracking Centre Forward). It checks the active phase PI record, team tactical mode, player/team side, time-slice state, position and distance conditions. This is a candidate-generation path, not merely a role label.
